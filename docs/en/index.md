## Understanding Reliability

- How often does your electricity service experience interruptions?
- How long do those interruptions last?
- What can public reporting tell you about your service?

This Explorer makes annual utility-reported reliability information easier to understand.

[**⚡️Open the Electricity Reliability Explorer**](https://civic-interconnect.github.io/explorer-electricity-reliability/app/)

## Indicators

The explorer presents three common reliability measurements:

- **SAIDI:** How **many minutes** of interruption the average customer
  experienced during a reporting year.
- **SAIFI:** How **many interruptions** the average customer experienced.
- **CAIDI:** How **long an interruption lasted on average**.

## Interpreting Results

Figures describe annual, utility-reported system averages.
They can't tell how long any specific household was interrupted,
can't predict outages, and don't indicate causes.

Reporting methods **differ between utilities** and
understanding distinctions is essential when interpreting
the numbers.

## Data Sources

Original data comes from the
[U.S. Energy Information Administration, Form EIA-861](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia861/).

Data is processed by
[Catalyst Cooperative through PUDL](https://github.com/catalyst-cooperative/pudl)
and reformatted for the explorer.

See the
[Data Card](https://github.com/civic-interconnect/explorer-electricity-reliability/blob/main/data/DATA_CARD.md)
for details.
