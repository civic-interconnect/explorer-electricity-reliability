# Glossary

Definitions of terms used in the Electricity Reliability Explorer.

## CAIDI

**Customer Average Interruption Duration Index.**

Average duration of a sustained customer interruption,
in minutes per interruption.

## Customer Count

The number of customers reported for the corresponding
utility, year, and reporting record.

Customer counts may change over time.

## EIA Utility ID

An identifier assigned by the U.S. Energy Information
Administration to a utility.

The explorer uses this identifier rather than relying
on utility names alone.

## IEEE Reporting Standard

A standardized approach to reporting electric
distribution reliability.

The source data identifies records reported under
the IEEE standard separately from other methods.

## Loss of Supply

An interruption associated with the loss of electricity
supply to the reporting utility or distribution system.

Some reported reliability measures exclude these events.

## Major Event Day

A day classified under the applicable reporting method
as involving unusually significant interruption activity.

Major event days are not necessarily synonymous with
declared emergencies or named weather events.

## PUDL

**Public Utility Data Liberation.**

An open-data project maintained by Catalyst Cooperative
that processes and publishes energy-sector data.

## SAIDI

**System Average Interruption Duration Index.**

Average total duration of sustained interruptions
per customer during a reporting year.

Unit: minutes per customer.

## SAIFI

**System Average Interruption Frequency Index.**

Average number of sustained interruptions per customer
during a reporting year.

Unit: interruptions per customer.

## Sustained Interruption

An interruption lasting longer than the threshold
specified by the applicable reporting standard.

Definitions and thresholds can vary by methodology.

## Utility

An organization identified in the EIA reporting records.

A utility may operate in more than one state, and
its reported name may change over time.

## Not Reported

A source value that is missing or unavailable.

A missing value must not be interpreted as zero.
